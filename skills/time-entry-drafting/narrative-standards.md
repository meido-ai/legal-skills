# Narrative standards (short version)

> **Customize this file.** Replace these defaults with your firm's rules. If the firm uses the `narrative-standards` skill, keep this file short and let that skill hold the full rules.

- Start with an action verb in the present tense: "Draft", "Review", "Revise", "Confer", "Analyze".
- Say what was worked on and why, specifically enough that the client understands the value: "Draft opposition to motion to compel production of maintenance records", not "Work on motion".
- One task per entry unless grouping is allowed. If several tasks are grouped, list each one.
- Name the other people involved by role or name when it helps: "Confer with client (J. Ortiz) regarding…".
- No vague phrases on their own: "attention to", "work on", "review file", "various", "follow up".
- No administrative or clerical tasks billed at attorney rates (scheduling, filing, copying, organizing files).
- No privileged detail beyond what the bill needs.
- Use the client's preferred terms and matter names.
