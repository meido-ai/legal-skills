# 20117 Northwind Insurance (fictional example): billing rules

> **This is a fictional example** to show how a filled-in rules file looks. Northwind Insurance is not a real client, and these rules are not from any real guidelines. Delete this file once you have real client files.

- **Source:** Northwind Insurance Outside Counsel Billing Guidelines, version 4, January 2026 (fictional)
- **Effective:** 2026-02-01
- **Applies to:** All litigation matters
- **Reviewed by:** [Relationship partner] on [date]
- **Billing increment:** 0.1 hour
- **Codes required:** UTBMS litigation task and activity codes on every entry

## Rules

| # | Rule | Level | Guidelines section |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Every entry has a UTBMS task code and activity code. | Block | 3.1 |
| 2 | No block billing: each entry describes one task with its own time. | Block | 3.2 |
| 3 | No more than 0.2 hours per day for internal conferences between firm personnel on a matter, unless approved in advance. | Fix | 3.4 |
| 4 | Only one timekeeper may bill for attending a deposition, hearing, or meeting unless approved in advance. | Fix | 3.5 |
| 5 | No billing for clerical tasks (scheduling, copying, filing, indexing, organizing files), at any rate. | Block | 3.6 |
| 6 | No billing for preparing invoices, responding to billing inquiries, or conflicts checks. | Block | 3.7 |
| 7 | Legal research over 3.0 hours on a single issue requires prior approval; note the approval in the narrative. | Warn | 3.8 |
| 8 | Narratives must not use "attention to", "work on", "review file", or "follow up" without a specific task and subject. | Fix | 3.3 |
| 9 | Time entered more than 60 days after the work date will not be paid. | Block | 2.4 |
| 10 | No time for training or onboarding new timekeepers on the matter. | Block | 3.9 |

## Rate and staffing rules

| # | Rule | Level | Guidelines section |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 12 | No more than one partner, one associate, and one paralegal per matter without approval. | Warn | 4.3 |

## Expense rules

| # | Rule | Level | Guidelines section |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 14 | Travel time is billed at 50% and must be noted as travel in the narrative. | Fix | 5.4 |
