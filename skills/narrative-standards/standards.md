# Firm narrative standards

> **Customize this file.** These are reasonable defaults. Replace them with your firm's written standards, and record who approved them and when.
>
> Approved by: _______ Date: _______

## Structure

- Start with a present-tense action verb: Draft, Revise, Review, Analyze, Research, Confer, Prepare, Attend, Correspond.
- Then the object: the specific document, issue, or event.
- Then the purpose, when it isn't obvious: "…to prepare for mediation", "…regarding deficiencies in production".
- One task per entry. If several tasks are grouped (where allowed), list each with a semicolon.

## Required detail

- Name documents specifically: "opposition to motion to compel", not "brief".
- Name people by role or name when it adds clarity: "confer with client (J. Ortiz)", "correspond with opposing counsel".
- For research, state the issue researched, not just "legal research".
- For calls and meetings, say who and what about.

## Not allowed

- Vague narratives on their own: "attention to", "work on", "review file", "follow up", "various", "misc.".
- Block billing of distinct tasks under one time value, unless the client allows it.
- Clerical or administrative tasks at professional rates: scheduling, filing, copying, indexing, organizing files, updating calendars.
- Internal firm administration: conflicts checks, billing, staffing discussions (unless a client agrees to pay for them).
- Privileged detail: legal advice, strategy, or quotes from privileged communications.
- Abbreviations a client reviewer won't know.

## Billing increments

- Default increment: 0.1 hour, rounded up. Change this if the firm or a client uses a different increment.
